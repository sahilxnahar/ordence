Where to send what

Same inbox either way — this just helps it get to the right person faster.

  • Sales and scoping

    What you sell, how many people, what is breaking. You will get a written scope and a number, usually within one business day.

  • Support, if you are already a customer

    By email, and by phone on the number in your agreement. Something that has stopped your business working gets looked at the same day.

  • Security

    Vulnerability reports and due-diligence questionnaires. Acknowledged within one business day. The security page says what is built and what is not.

  • Privacy and data requests

    Access, correction, erasure, or withdrawing consent. Answered within thirty days, and you do not need a lawyer, a format or a reason.

What to expect

A person reads it, usually the same day. If the answer needs thought you will get an acknowledgement first rather than silence, and you will not be handed between three people who each ask you to explain it again.

We are a small team, which means occasionally you will wait a few hours longer than you would from a company with a follow-the-sun desk. It also means the person replying can actually change the thing you are asking about.
