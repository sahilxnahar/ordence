Why there is no price list on this page

Because it would be a lie by omission. Two businesses with the same headcount can be four times apart in what it actually takes to get them running: one has clean data in one spreadsheet, the other has eleven years of history across four systems and a process that only one person understands.

A public per-user price would have to be either high enough to cover the second case — in which case the first business is overpaying — or low enough for the first, in which case the second gets a change order three weeks in. Both are the same trick. We would rather spend a day understanding your business and give you a number we can hold.

What the number covers

Everything in this list is inside the quoted price. If something is not in the quote, it is not in the price, and it will be named on the scope sheet as excluded rather than left ambiguous.

  • The modules on your scope, for the number of users on your scope.
  • Hosting, backups, security updates and every platform improvement we ship.
  • Configuration — your fields, your stages, your document templates, your rules.
  • Support from the people who built it, not a tier-one queue.
  • Your data, exportable in open formats, whenever you want it, without asking.

What is quoted separately

Named up front, priced up front. None of these are surprises discovered after signature.

  • Migrating history out of your existing systems, once we have seen the data.
  • Bespoke development for something your industry needs and the platform does not have yet.
  • Integrations with third-party services — payment gateways, logistics, marketplaces.
  • Website and design work, which is its own scope and its own quote.
  • On-site training, if you want it. Remote is included.

Questions people actually ask

Is it per user?

Partly. Users are one input into the number, but a ten-person business with a complicated warehouse costs more to run than a thirty-person one selling a simple catalogue. Quoting purely per seat would misprice both.

Is there a contract?

Yes, and the terms are on this site in plain English before you talk to anyone. There is an initial term, after which it renews for the same length automatically unless either side gives written notice before the renewal date. We send a reminder before that window closes, because a renewal you did not notice is not one you agreed to.

What if we need something halfway through?

Small changes — a field, a report, a rule — are part of the subscription and we do not bill for them. Anything larger gets a written change note with a price on it, before we build it.

Can we start with one module?

Yes, and it is usually the better path. Start where it hurts most, get people actually using it, then add. A big-bang rollout across every department at once is how these projects fail.

What happens if we leave?

You export everything yourself, in open formats, at any time — including while an invoice is overdue. After the end date you have thirty days to get anything else out, and then we delete our copies. On money: fees paid for a term you leave part-way through are not refunded, because our costs for that term are already committed; if we are the ones ending it, the unused part comes back to you. All of it is in the terms rather than left to goodwill.
