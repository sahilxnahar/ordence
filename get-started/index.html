What to send

Five lines is enough to start. Send it to hello@ordence.com and we will come back the same day, usually with a time to talk it through properly.

  • What you sell, and roughly how you sell it.
  • How many people, and how many of them would use the system.
  • What you use today — including if the honest answer is spreadsheets and WhatsApp.
  • What is breaking. The thing that made you look for this in the first place.
  • Anything with a date on it — a compliance deadline, a system being switched off, a growth plan that current systems will not survive.

What happens next

Four steps, and you can stop after any of them.

  1. A conversation, usually within a day

    Thirty to forty-five minutes. Mostly us asking questions about how work actually moves through your business, not us presenting slides.

  2. A written scope

    Modules, users, what we migrate, what we build, and — importantly — what we are not doing. Plus a fixed price. Yours to keep and to circulate internally whether or not you go ahead.

  3. A walkthrough on your own data

    Where it is useful, we load a sample of your real records so you are looking at your own customers rather than a demo tenant full of invented ones.

  4. Then you decide

    If it is not right, tell us. We would rather hear no than manage a project that should not have started.

What we will not do

We will not put you through a qualification script before anyone will talk about price. We will not send a proposal that leaves the number for the last page and makes it conditional. We will not chase you weekly after you have said no.

And if what you actually need is a spreadsheet and a better process, we will tell you that too. It has happened more than once and it costs us nothing worth having.

Start with one line.

"We are forty people selling building materials and our stock numbers are never right." That is genuinely enough.
